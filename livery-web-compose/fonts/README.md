Roboto Flex is bundled from rc-players' `rc-player/wasm/dist-assets/fonts/RobotoFlex.ttf`
at commit `adde1014109cced19f70551764b3604fad55c50b`.
It is distributed under the accompanying SIL Open Font License. The catalog prepares it before
mounting players; the synchronous typeface loader never fetches during composition or drawing.

Noto Sans Symbols 2 comes from Debian fonts-noto-core (20201225-2), under the accompanying
SIL Open Font License. Its preloaded glyphs provide fallback for symbols absent from Roboto Flex,
including the sample banner’s U+2715 close glyph. No system-font or Google Fonts fetch is needed.
